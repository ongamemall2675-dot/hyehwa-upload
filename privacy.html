Privacy Policy — Hyehwa Upload (혜화야담 업로드)

Effective: October 10, 2026

1. Who uses this app

Hyehwa Upload is a personal desktop tool used only by the operator of the «Hyehwa Yadam» channels to upload their own videos to their own accounts. It has no public users.

2. Information used

  • Basic profile of the operator's own accounts (account/channel name, ID, avatar) — to show which account will receive the upload.
  • The videos, images, titles, captions and schedule times the operator chooses on their own PC.
  • Access tokens issued by YouTube (Google), Meta (Instagram/Facebook) and TikTok after the operator signs in.

This information is used only to upload the operator's content to the operator's own accounts.

3. Storage and sharing

  • Tokens and settings are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are not sent to any server of ours (we run none).
  • No information is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, except to the platform that receives the upload.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.

4. Revoking access / deleting data

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Meta: Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (Manage app permissions)
  • Deleting the app folder on the operator's PC removes all stored tokens and data.

5. Platform policies

This app uses the YouTube API Services (YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy), the Meta Graph API and the TikTok for Developers APIs (TikTok Terms of Service).
