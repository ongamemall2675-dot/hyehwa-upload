Hyehwa Upload · 혜화야담 업로드

Hyehwa Upload is a personal desktop tool used by one creator — the operator of the «Hyehwa Yadam (혜화야담)» channels — to upload and schedule their own videos to their own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook Pages and TikTok.

It runs only on the creator's own Windows PC (a local program plus a Chrome extension). It is not offered to the public, has no user sign-up, and does not collect data from other people.

What it does

  • The creator picks a finished video, title, caption and publish time on their PC.
  • The tool signs in with the creator's own account (official login of each platform) and uploads the video to that account.
  • For TikTok it uses Login Kit and the Content Posting API to post the creator's videos to the creator's own TikTok account, with the privacy setting the creator chooses.
